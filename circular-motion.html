一、角度的新單位：弧度Part 1 · A new unit of angle: the radian

弧度的定義What is a radian?

在半徑為 \(r\) 的圓周上，弧長 \(s\) 與半徑 \(r\) 之比定義為圓心角 \(\theta\)，單位是弧度（radian, \(\mathrm{rad}\)）：On a circle of radius \(r\), the angle \(\theta\) subtended at the centre by an arc of length \(s\) can be measured in radians, defined as:

\(\theta=\dfrac{s}{r}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=r\theta\)
當弧長等於半徑（\(s=r\)）時，圓心角 \(\theta=1\ \mathrm{rad}\)。When the arc length equals the radius (\(s=r\)), the angle is \(\theta=1\ \mathrm{rad}\).

弧度是兩個長度的比值，所以沒有量綱；單位記作 \(\mathrm{rad}\)。A radian is a ratio of two lengths, so it is dimensionless; its unit is written \(\mathrm{rad}\).

二、角速率、線性速率與公式推導Part 2 · Angular speed, linear speed and derivations

角速率Angular speed

角速率 \(\omega\)（omega）是每單位時間轉過的角位移 \(\theta\)：Angular speed \(\omega\) (omega) is the angular displacement \(\theta\) turned through per unit time:

\(\omega=\dfrac{\theta}{t}\)
單位：\(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)（弧度每秒）Unit: \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\) (radians per second)
計算時 \(\theta\) 必須以弧度為單位！\(\theta\) must be in radians!

勻速圓周運動中，\(\omega\) 保持不變。In uniform circular motion, \(\omega\) is constant.

淺橙色扇形 = 過去 \(1\ \mathrm{s}\) 內轉過的角度，數值上等於 \(\omega\)。Shaded sector = angle swept in the last \(1\ \mathrm{s}\), numerically equal to \(\omega\).

二、角速率、線性速率與公式推導Part 2 · Angular speed, linear speed and derivations

角速率：例子Angular speed: examples

例 1　時鐘的秒針每 \(60\ \mathrm{s}\) 轉一圈。Ex 1　A clock's second hand turns once every \(60\ \mathrm{s}\).
\(\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{60}\approx0.105\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)
例 2　風扇每分鐘轉 \(300\) 圈。Ex 2　A fan turns at \(300\) revolutions per minute.
\(\omega=\dfrac{300\times2\pi}{60}=10\pi\approx31.4\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)
例 3　地球每 \(24\ \mathrm{h}\) 自轉一圈。Ex 3　The Earth spins once every \(24\ \mathrm{h}\).
\(\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{24\times3600}\approx7.27\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)
例 4　一物體在 \(2\ \mathrm{s}\) 內轉過 \(90^\circ\)。Ex 4　An object turns through \(90^\circ\) in \(2\ \mathrm{s}\).
先把 \(90^\circ\) 轉成 \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\ \mathrm{rad}\)：First convert \(90^\circ\) to \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\ \mathrm{rad}\): \(\omega=\dfrac{\pi/2}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\approx0.785\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)

週期 \(T\) 與頻率 \(f\)Period \(T\) and frequency \(f\)

  • 週期 \(T\)：轉一圈所需的時間（\(\mathrm{s}\)）Period \(T\): time for one revolution (\(\mathrm{s}\))
  • 頻率 \(f\)：每秒轉的圈數（\(\mathrm{Hz}\)）Frequency \(f\): revolutions per second (\(\mathrm{Hz}\))
\(\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=2\pi f\;\Longrightarrow\;T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}\)

\(f=\dfrac{1}{T}\)

推導：轉動一圈需時 \(T\)，角位移 \(\theta=2\pi\)，代入 \(\omega=\dfrac{\theta}{t}\) 即得。例 1 就是 \(T=60\ \mathrm{s}\) 的情況。Derivation: one revolution takes time \(T\) with angular displacement \(\theta=2\pi\); substitute into \(\omega=\dfrac{\theta}{t}\). Ex 1 is the case \(T=60\ \mathrm{s}\).

二、角速率、線性速率與公式推導Part 2 · Angular speed, linear speed and derivations

線性速率與角速率的關係Linear speed and angular speed

時間 \(t\) 內移動的弧長 \(s=r\theta\)，故：In time \(t\) the object moves an arc length \(s=r\theta\), so:

\(v=\dfrac{s}{t}=\dfrac{r\theta}{t}=\boxed{\,r\omega\,}=\dfrac{2\pi r}{T}\)

\(\omega\) 必須用 \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)。\(\omega\) must be in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).

同一轉盤上各點的 \(\omega\) 相同，但離圓心愈遠，\(v\) 愈大（\(v\propto r\)）。All points on a rotating disc share the same \(\omega\), but the farther from the centre, the greater \(v\) (\(v\propto r\)).
例　單車車輪半徑 \(0.35\ \mathrm{m}\)，以 \(20\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\) 轉動。求輪邊一點的速率。Ex　A bicycle wheel of radius \(0.35\ \mathrm{m}\) turns at \(20\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\). Find the speed of a point on the rim.
\(v=r\omega=0.35\times20=7\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

三、向心加速度與向心力Part 3 · Centripetal acceleration and centripetal force

速率不變，速度卻不斷改變Constant speed, changing velocity

  • 勻速圓周運動：速率 \(v\) 不變。Uniform circular motion: the speed \(v\) is constant.
  • 但運動方向（沿切線）時刻在改變。But the direction of motion (along the tangent) changes all the time.
  • 速度是矢量（有大小和方向）：方向改變 ⇒ 速度改變。Velocity is a vector (magnitude and direction): direction changes ⇒ velocity changes.
速度改變 ⇒ 有加速度Velocity changes ⇒ acceleration \(\;\vec a=\dfrac{\Delta\vec v}{\Delta t}\)
\(\Delta\vec v=\vec v_2-\vec v_1\) 總是平行於「弧的中點 → 圓心」的方向。當 \(\Delta\theta\to0\)，這就是該點加速度的方向：指向圓心。\(\Delta\vec v=\vec v_2-\vec v_1\) is always parallel to the line from the arc's midpoint to the centre. As \(\Delta\theta\to0\) this becomes the direction of the acceleration at that point: towards the centre.

左：圓周上兩點的速度；右：矢量三角形 \(\Delta\vec v=\vec v_2-\vec v_1\)Left: velocities at two points on the circle; right: the vector triangle \(\Delta\vec v=\vec v_2-\vec v_1\)

三、向心加速度與向心力Part 3 · Centripetal acceleration and centripetal force

向心加速度Centripetal acceleration

\(a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=r\omega^2\)

代入 \(v=r\omega\)：Substituting \(v=r\omega\): \(a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{(r\omega)^2}{r}=r\omega^2\)

方向：時刻指向圓心（「向心」即指向中心）。單位：\(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)Direction: always towards the centre ("centripetal" means centre-seeking). Unit: \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)
推導（選讀）Derivation (optional)
由上一頁的相似三角形：From the similar triangles on the previous page: \(\dfrac{|\Delta\vec v|}{v}\approx\dfrac{\Delta s}{r}\;\Rightarrow\;a_c=\dfrac{|\Delta\vec v|}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{v}{r}\cdot\dfrac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{v^2}{r}\)
例　汽車以 \(15\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) 駛過半徑 \(50\ \mathrm{m}\) 的彎道，求向心加速度。Ex　A car goes round a bend of radius \(50\ \mathrm{m}\) at \(15\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\). Find its centripetal acceleration.
\(a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{15^2}{50}=4.5\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) ，指向彎道的圓心, towards the centre of the bend

三、向心加速度與向心力Part 3 · Centripetal acceleration and centripetal force

向心力Centripetal force

由牛頓第二定律 \(F=ma\)：By Newton's second law \(F=ma\):

\(F_c=ma_c=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}=mr\omega^2\)
方向：與 \(\vec a_c\) 相同，指向圓心。Direction: same as \(\vec a_c\), towards the centre.
DSE 隔離體圖鐵律DSE free-body diagram rule
向心力不是額外的新力，而是現有真實力（摩擦力、張力、重力、法向力）指向圓心的合力。繪畫隔離體圖時，嚴禁畫出「向心力 \(F_c\)」或「離心力」箭矢。Centripetal force is not an extra force: it is the resultant of the real forces (friction, tension, gravity, normal force) towards the centre. In a free-body diagram, never draw an arrow for "centripetal force \(F_c\)" or "centrifugal force".
例　質量 \(0.2\ \mathrm{kg}\) 的小球以長 \(0.5\ \mathrm{m}\) 的繩子在光滑水平桌面上作勻速圓周運動，速率為 \(4\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)。求繩子的拉力。若速率加倍呢？Ex　A \(0.2\ \mathrm{kg}\) ball on a \(0.5\ \mathrm{m}\) string moves in uniform circular motion on a smooth horizontal table at \(4\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\). Find the tension. What if the speed doubles?
\(F_c=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}=\dfrac{0.2\times4^2}{0.5}=6.4\ \mathrm{N}\)；速率加倍，\(F\propto v^2\)，拉力變為 \(4\) 倍 \(=25.6\ \mathrm{N}\)doubling the speed: \(F\propto v^2\), so the tension becomes \(4\) times \(=25.6\ \mathrm{N}\)
